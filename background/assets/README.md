# Harbor artwork

`harbor.png` is an original background generated with the built-in image generation tool for Star Isle Fisher. It is embedded once in the controller executable and is not read or regenerated per cast. No CELESTAY artwork or font files were extracted.

Prompt: Original cozy 16-bit seaside pixel art; blue sky, cream clouds, quiet turquoise ocean, a tall wooden fishing house on the right with terracotta awnings, plants and a small dock; open space on the left for readable controls. Crisp pixels, warm wood and seafoam palette, bright morning light; no text, logo or interface.

## Fisher icon

`fisher.png` was redrawn with the built-in image generation tool from the user's supplied fisherman screenshot. `fisher.ico` is a multi-resolution encoding of that transparent artwork for Windows; PNG-to-ICO conversion does not change its composition.

Prompt: Isolate and redraw the seated pixel fisherman from the supplied reference, preserving a tan straw hat, navy clothes, brown boots and a curved fishing rod. Large legible centered silhouette, crisp pixels, dark outline, transparent background, no sky, house, dock, text or frame.
